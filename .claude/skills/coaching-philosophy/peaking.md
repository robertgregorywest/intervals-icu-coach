# Peaking — the last four weeks before an A race

The build-block rules assume months of runway. Inside four weeks of an A race the goal turns from
building the engine to delivering the one that exists, and the build rules invert:

- **The middle-band dose becomes a floor, not the watch metric.** Hold ~1.5–2 h/wk in the 76–106%
  band so the engine does not hollow out; judge the week on race-specific delivery — race-pace reps
  held at watts and cadence, and the start.
- **The key session is supra-threshold race-pace work.** "Sustained sessions sit under threshold"
  still governs the sustained session ([structure.md](structure.md)); race pace sits on top of the
  in-band floor, not in place of it.
- **Every benchmark is also a race-specific session.** A 20-min test, a late-ride durability read or a
  TT discriminator costs a hard day and several recovering ones for a number that changes nothing
  before the race; they wait until after it.

## Taper

- **Taper removes volume, never intensity** — two touches of race intensity every week, race week
  included.
- **Two stages, 14 days**: a reduce week at ~−15% volume, then race week at −50% or more. A deeper
  cut lands race day below the fitness it was meant to carry: the Nationals 2026 taper unloaded long,
  CTL fell ~10%, and the fresh opening was paid for with a collapsing back half.
- **Last heavy strength ≥10 days out**; one light neural session (jumps, light squats, nothing
  grinding) early in race week, then nothing.
- **Openers the day before the event**, not two days before.
