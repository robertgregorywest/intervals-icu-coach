Pre-race opener. Long steady base, one threshold block, then two short supra-threshold efforts and a single sprint to sharpen without cost. The intent is neuromuscular readiness, not stimulus — if it leaves you tired, it was ridden too hard.

- Warm up 20m 54-66%
- Tempo 10m 72-88%
- 10m 59-71%
- Threshold 5m 90-110%
- 10m 54-66%
- Opener 1m 113-138%
- 3m 54-66%
- Opener 1m 113-138%
- 3m 54-66%
- Sprint 0m10s 225-275%
- Cooldown 10m Z1
