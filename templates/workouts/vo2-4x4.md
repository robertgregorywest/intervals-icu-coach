Classic VO₂max session: 4 min hard with equal recovery. Self-paced inside the band: the highest power you can repeat, final rep ≥ first rep. 78–85% MAP is a starting anchor for a rider whose FTP:MAP is low; recalibrate it to 90–95% of a 5-min test once one exists. (Seiler et al., 2013; Stern, CycleCoach 2025.)

- Warm-up 15m 50-60%

4x
  - On 4m 78-85%
  - Off 4m 50%

- Cooldown 10m 45%
