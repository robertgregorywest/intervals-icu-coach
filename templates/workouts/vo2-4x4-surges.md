Same mean power as `vo2-4x4`, redistributed: three 30 s surges to ~MAP inside each 4-min rep, with settles between. In trained cyclists this raised time above 90% VO₂max at matched mean power (Bossi et al., 2020; acute lab data, one study). The surges aren't sprints — ride them at MAP, not above. Heart rate won't show the difference; judge it on power.

- Warm-up 15m 50-60%

4x
  - Surge 30s 95-100%
  - Settle 1m 70-75%
  - Surge 30s 95-100%
  - Settle 1m 70-75%
  - Surge 30s 95-100%
  - Settle 30s 70-75%
  - Off 4m 50%

- Cooldown 10m 45%
