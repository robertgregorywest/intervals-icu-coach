Each series opens with a 2-min preload to drive VO₂ up, then 30 s easy, so the 30/15s start near VO₂peak instead of climbing into it (Vaccari et al., 2020). Self-paced: the highest power you can repeat across every series; the band is the floor. The preload is work, not warm-up. Bands assume a rider whose ramp MAP overstates 5-min power; a diesel rides the reps nearer 100% MAP. (Rønnestad et al., 2015, 2020; Odden et al., 2024.)

- Warm-up 15m 50-60%

Series 3x
  - Preload 2m 90-95% 95rpm
  - Short rec 30s 50%
  8x
    - On 30s 88-95%
    - Off 15s 50%
  - Series rec 3m 50%

- Cooldown 10m 45%
